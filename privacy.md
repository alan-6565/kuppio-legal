# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 26, 2026 · **Last updated:** October 29, 2026

Kuppio ("Kuppio," "we," "us," or "our") is the Kuppio mobile app, owned and operated by Jahir Crisostomo. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Kuppio, you agree to the practices described here.

## Information We Collect

**Account information.** When you sign up, we collect your email address and password (stored securely and never in plain text) to create and secure your account.

**Location.** With your permission, we collect your device's GPS location to show you nearby spots, calculate distances, and center the map near you. You can deny or revoke location access at any time in your device settings; the app will still work, using an approximate default location instead.

**Photos and media.** If you choose to add a photo or video — to a review, a post, or a business listing — we collect and store that file. If you submit a business for verification, we also collect a photo of a government-issued ID and a photo of the business/storefront; these two verification photos are stored privately and are only visible to you and to Kuppio's review team, never to other users.

**Content you create.** Reviews, posts, comments, saved spots, collections, and business listings you create are stored so they can be shown to you and (where public-facing, like reviews and posts) to other users.

**Camera and microphone.** If you record a video post, we access your camera and microphone only for that recording, with your permission.

**Orders.** When you place an order ahead, we store the items, total, pickup time, any tip, and any note you add, and share them with that business so they can prepare it.

**Payments.** If you pay for an order in the app, your card is entered into a payment form provided by Stripe, our payment processor. Your card details go directly to Stripe; Kuppio never sees or stores your full card number. We store a record of each payment (the amounts, whether it was paid or refunded, and Stripe's reference for it) so we can show your order history and handle refunds. Stripe's use of your information is described in the [Stripe Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).

**Businesses that take payments.** If you connect a business to Stripe to get paid, Stripe collects the information it needs to verify you and pay you out (such as your name, date of birth, address, the last digits of your Social Security number, and your bank account) on its own pages. Kuppio receives only what's needed to run payments for your business, like your Stripe account's reference and whether it can take payments, plus the business details we pass to Stripe to start your setup (your business's name, its Kuppio page, and your email).

**Messages.** When you send a direct message, we store it so the people in the conversation can read it. Messages are private to the conversation. Kuppio's review team can see a message only if someone reports it. Deleting your account deletes your conversations.

**Push notifications.** If you allow notifications, we store a push token for your device so we can send you alerts about your orders, replies to your reviews, new followers, and business applications. The token is removed when you sign out or delete your account, and you can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

**Crash reports.** If the app crashes or hits an error, we collect a crash report: the error, the screens and buttons used just before it, and technical details like your device model, operating system and app version. Crash reports don't include your name, email, location, or what you typed, and we use them only to fix problems.

**Usage and device information.** We may collect basic technical information (such as device type and app version) to diagnose problems and keep the app working correctly.

## How We Use Information

We use the information above to:
- Operate core features: showing nearby spots, displaying and ranking reviews/posts, letting you save spots, place preorders, pay for them, and message other users
- Process payments and refunds, and pay businesses for their orders
- Verify business ownership claims before granting control of a business listing
- Review reports of content or users that violate our guidelines
- Maintain and improve the app, and diagnose technical issues

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

## How We Store and Share Information

Your data is stored using Supabase, our backend infrastructure provider, which hosts our database and file storage. Push notifications are delivered through Expo's push notification service and Apple/Google's notification systems, which receive only the notification's text and your device's push token. Crash reports are processed by Sentry. These providers act on our behalf and do not use your data for their own purposes.

Payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe receives your payment details directly when you pay, and, for businesses, the information needed to verify and pay them. Stripe is responsible for that information under its own privacy policy.

Reviews, posts, comments, and public business listings are visible to other users of the app, as is inherent to how those features work. When you place an order, the business sees your order, your username, and your note. Your government-ID and business-verification photos are never shown publicly — only to Kuppio's review team, for the sole purpose of confirming a business claim.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Kuppio, our users, or the public.

## Your Choices

- **Delete your account.** You can permanently delete your account and associated personal data at any time from Settings → Delete account in the app. Businesses you manage are unclaimed (not deleted) so the listing can continue to exist for other users; your personal reviews, posts, comments, messages, and saved items are removed. We keep records of paid orders where the law requires us to (for example, for tax and accounting).
- **Block and report.** You can block another user to stop seeing their content, and report content or users that violate our guidelines.
- **Location permission.** You can deny or revoke location access at any time in your device's Settings app.

## Children's Privacy

Kuppio is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us using the information below and we will delete it.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
