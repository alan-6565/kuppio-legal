# Kuppio

**Discover the small food spots that get overlooked: home bakers, pop-ups, food trucks, and cafés.**

Kuppio is a mobile app for finding local food businesses and ordering ahead from them. Customers browse nearby spots, watch short videos and read reviews, then order for pickup. Businesses get a storefront page, a menu, and a simple way to take orders.

## How ordering works

1. Find a spot and add items from its menu.
2. Choose a pickup time and how to pay: in the app, or at pickup (each business chooses which it accepts).
3. The business accepts your order and lets you know when it's ready.

## Payments

Payments in the app are processed securely by [Stripe](https://stripe.com) on behalf of each business; card details go straight to Stripe and Kuppio never stores them. Customers pay the menu price, an optional tip (100% to the business), and a card-processing fee shown before paying. From November 28, 2026, businesses pay Kuppio a 5% fee on the menu total of orders paid in the app. There's no fee on tips or on orders paid at pickup. See the [Seller Agreement](seller-agreement).

## Cancellations and refunds

- Customers can cancel until the business accepts the order (for businesses that accept orders automatically, within 5 minutes).
- Businesses can decline an order within 5 minutes of receiving it.
- Orders a business doesn't accept within 30 minutes are cancelled automatically.
- Paid orders that are cancelled or declined are refunded in full, usually within 5–10 business days.
- For other problems with an order, contact the business first, or email us.
