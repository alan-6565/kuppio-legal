# Seller Agreement

**Version:** October 19, 2026

This Seller Agreement ("Agreement") applies to anyone who lists a business on the Kuppio mobile app ("Kuppio," "we," "us," or "our") and takes orders through it ("you," the "Seller"). Kuppio is owned and operated by Jahir Crisostomo. You accept this Agreement when you apply to list a new business or claim an existing listing. It adds to the [Terms of Service](terms) and [Privacy Policy](privacy), which also apply to you.

## 1. Your Listing

You must own the business you list or be authorized to act for it. You're responsible for keeping your listing accurate: name, location or service area, hours, menu, prices, photos, ingredients, and whether items are available. Kuppio reviews every application, but approval doesn't mean we've checked or endorse your products.

## 2. Fees

Kuppio doesn't charge Sellers any fees right now. If we start charging fees, we'll tell you at least 30 days before they take effect, and they'll only apply to orders placed after that date. You can stop selling on Kuppio at any time.

## 3. Orders, Payment and Refunds

Orders placed through Kuppio are pay-at-pickup. Kuppio sends the order to you, but does not process payment; the customer pays you directly. That means:

- You set your prices, and you collect payment.
- You decide whether to accept or decline an order, and you're responsible for having accepted orders ready at the pickup time you confirmed.
- Refunds, exchanges and order problems are between you and the customer. Please handle them fairly and promptly.
- If you can't fill an order you accepted, cancel or decline it in the app as soon as you can so the customer knows.

You're responsible for any taxes, business licenses and record-keeping your sales require.

## 4. Food Safety and Permits

You must follow the food safety, labeling and licensing laws that apply where you prepare and sell food. This includes:

- Preparing food in a kitchen that is allowed for what you sell, under the permit or registration your county or state requires.
- Home-based Sellers: selling only what your home kitchen permit allows (for example, a California Cottage Food Operation or Microenterprise Home Kitchen Operation permit), within its sales limits.
- Listing major allergens and ingredients accurately when customers need them, and keeping food at safe temperatures until pickup.

Home-based Sellers can add a permit number to their listing. If you do, it must be a real, current permit for your business, and the "Permitted home kitchen" badge is shown based on what you enter. The permit number is shown publicly on your listing so customers can look it up. We may ask for proof of your permit and remove the badge or listing if it's not valid.

## 5. Prohibited Items

You may not sell or offer through Kuppio:

- Alcohol, cannabis or CBD products, tobacco or vaping products
- Raw (unpasteurized) milk or dairy, or any food your permit or local law doesn't allow you to sell
- Prescription or over-the-counter drugs, supplements making health claims, or anything marketed as treating a medical condition
- Weapons, live animals, counterfeit goods, or anything illegal where you or the customer are located
- Items that aren't food or drink, except merchandise from your own business (like mugs or tote bags)

## 6. Conduct

Treat customers respectfully. Don't post fake reviews, offer rewards for reviews, review your own business, or use customer information (like names or phone numbers from orders) for anything other than filling that order.

## 7. Removal and Ending This Agreement

We may hide or remove a listing, remove a badge, or suspend selling if you break this Agreement, if customers report serious problems (like food safety concerns), or if the law requires it. You can end this Agreement at any time by turning off ordering or deleting your account.

## 8. Responsibility

You're responsible for the food and items you sell and for claims that arise from them. Kuppio is a platform that connects you with customers; it isn't the seller, preparer or deliverer of any order. The disclaimers and limitation of liability in the Terms of Service also apply to this Agreement.

## 9. Changes

We may update this Agreement. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect. Continuing to sell on Kuppio after the change means you accept the updated Agreement.
